Who we are

We turn your data foundation into a Composable Customer Data Platform — then drive engagement with agentic AI and leading CDP platforms.

Traditional packaged CDPs copy your customer data into a separate silo you don't fully control. A composable approach keeps the data on the foundation you already own — and assembles the right best-of-breed components on top of it.

Kyper Tech takes you there end to end. We ideate the data foundation, strategize the architecture, build the platform, and activate it — across a System of Insight that knows your customer and a System of Engagement that acts on that knowledge. To power engagement, we integrate agentic AI and partner with leading off-the-shelf CDP platforms — using whatever drives results, without locking you in.
